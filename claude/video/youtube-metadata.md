## Channel description

```
Ballistic Calculator 2 is a free, open-source ballistic calculator for Windows, Linux and macOS. The videos here show how to use it, one step at a time: a first trajectory, weather and wind, spin drift, Coriolis, scope reticles and BDC, measured drag curves, shooting at an angle, and building your own drag table. The calculations come from the open-source BallisticCalculator library for .NET. It is a full model of the bullet's flight, but still a model: always check it against your own shots.

## Episode 10

**Title:** Your own drag table — Ballistic Calculator 2, ep. 10

**Description:**

```
No radar drag file for your bullet? The program can build an approximate one, in two ways. From several ballistic coefficients: five G7 numbers taken off Lapua's radar curve for the 175 grain Scenar-L give a curve within one inch of the radar file at 1000 yards, where the single box BC was off by ten inches. And from velocities measured down range: Warner Tool publishes Doppler radar sheets for its Flatline bullets, and the .338 285 grain table, loaded from a small CSV file, rebuilds the curve — as long as you enter the air it was measured in. Warner tests at EMRTC, New Mexico Tech, near Socorro, New Mexico, high in the desert. The last episode of the series.
