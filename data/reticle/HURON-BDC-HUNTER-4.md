# HURON-BDC-HUNTER-4 — Trijicon Huron 1-4×24, BDC Hunter Holds

Trijicon's hunting BDC: a duplex crosshair with ticks at ±5 and ±10 MOA and **three hold bars** below the
centre. This is the **1-4×24** cut (**HR424-C-2700001**).

**Second focal plane.** Trijicon's sheet notes "Measurements shown at highest magnification", so these
subtensions are the **4×** ones and hold only at that setting.

## The pattern

- **Fine cross** — **0.5 MOA** lines, out to the post shoulders at **15 MOA**.
- **Heavy duplex posts** — **2.5 MOA** thick, from 15 MOA outward on the left, right and bottom arms. The
  vertical arm above centre stays fine; there is no top post.
- **Ticks** on the horizontal at **±5 and ±10 MOA**, 2.5 MOA tall.
- **Three hold bars** below the centre.

## The marks

| Drop below centre | Hold |
|---|---|
| 2.24 MOA | first |
| 5.00 MOA | second |
| 8.32 MOA | third |

All three printed on the sheet. Trijicon does not say what ranges they are for, nor which load — this is a
hunting reticle sold on the idea that you work out your own holds. The application labels them from your
trajectory, which is exactly what makes it useful here.

## The Huron family: one reticle, six cuts

The same BDC Hunter Holds pattern ships on six Huron models, and the **holds are the same on all of
them** — 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 MOA, with 15 MOA post shoulders and ±5 / ±10 MOA ticks throughout. What Trijicon
changes per model is **stroke weight**, because a 1-4× needs a heavier reticle than a 4-16×:

| Model | Scope | Holds | Post / tick | Fine line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HR424-C-2700001 | 1-4×24 | 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 | 2.5 MOA | 0.5 MOA |
| HR624-C-2700008 | 1-6×24 | 2.22 / 5 / 8.29 | 2.5 MOA | 0.5 MOA |
| HR940-C-2700006 | 3-9×40 | 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 | 1 MOA | 0.25 MOA |
| HR1040-C-2700002 | 2.5-10×40 | 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 | 1 MOA | 0.2 MOA |
| HR1240-C-2700003 | 3-12×40 | 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 | 1 MOA | 0.2 MOA |
| HR1650-C-2700009 | 4-16×50 | 2.24 / 5 / 8.32 | 1 MOA | 0.2 MOA |

Three of those are in this folder: this one, [HURON-BDC-HUNTER-6](HURON-BDC-HUNTER-6.md) and
[HURON-BDC-HUNTER-9](HURON-BDC-HUNTER-9.md). Since the holds are identical, any of them gives the same
BDC behaviour — pick the one whose line weight matches your scope. The remaining three models are the
0.2 MOA variant, which the 940 file approximates closely.

Note the 3-9×40 body also ships with a **German #4** (HR940-C-2700004) and a **Standard Duplex**
(HR940-C-2700005); only `-2700006` is this reticle.

## Nothing was measured

The sheet is stamped "**\*Reticles not drawn to scale**". Every dimension above is printed. The one value
that is not printed is the **hold bars' width**, drawn here at 2.6 MOA to match the sheet's proportions —
approximate, and cosmetic rather than a subtension you would measure with.

The posts taper where they meet the fine lines on the real etching; that taper is not reproduced.
