# VCOG-16-556-55gr — Trijicon VCOG 1-6×24, 5.56 segmented-circle BDC

Trijicon's segmented-circle-and-crosshair BDC as fitted to the 5.56 VCOG: four red arc segments
surrounding a wide red centre crosshair, with a ladder of horizontal stadia below. **First focal plane**,
so the subtensions hold at every magnification; framed as Trijicon's own sheet draws it, at **6×**.

## One drawing, two part numbers

Trijicon publishes a per-part reticle sheet for each VCOG. The sheets for **VC16-C-1600000 (.223 / 55 gr)**
and **VC16-C-1600001 (.223 / 77 gr)** are *different files* — different URLs, different checksums — whose
**rendered content is byte-for-byte identical**. Same ladder, same stadia, same numerals.

That means this one file is what Trijicon documents for both 5.56 part numbers. Either the two models
genuinely share a reticle, or Trijicon reused one drawing; the sheets cannot tell you which, and no drop
table is published for either. A 55 gr load and a 77 gr load do not share a trajectory, so if the two
optics really are etched differently, that difference is not in the public documentation.

It matters less than it might: the application labels every mark from **your** trajectory, so entering a
77 gr load simply relabels these stadia with the ranges that load actually reaches.

## The pattern

- **Segmented circle** — four filled red arcs, **66.4 MOA between the outer corners** (which is 19 in at
  25 m), with **17.8 MOA gaps** across both axes so the arcs stop clear of the crosshair and the ladder.
- **Centre crosshair** — red, **16.6 MOA wide**, which is **19 in at 100 m**, and the 100 m zero. Line
  thickness **1.0 MOA**.
- **BDC stadia** — six horizontal bars below the centre, each **19 in wide at its own range**. Numerals
  are hundreds of metres (4, 6, 8 etched).

## Which numbers came from where

Printed on the sheet: the 16.6 MOA crosshair, the 1.0 MOA line thickness, the 17.8 MOA gaps, the 66.4 MOA
outer span, and the three notes that define the whole scheme — *"BDC stadia are in 100's of meters"*,
*"Horizontal BDC stadia corresponds to 19″ wide target at that distance"*, *"Center x-hair width
corresponds to 19″ target at 100m"*.

**Trijicon publishes no drop table**, so each stadia's vertical position is measured off the sheet. What
makes that safe is that every position is corroborated by an independent quantity — its own **width**. Under
the printed 19 in rule a stadia's width alone gives its range, and the measured widths came out at 5.53,
4.17, 3.23, 2.80, 2.44 and 2.08 MOA, i.e. 300 / 398 / 513 / 592 / 679 / 796 m against the etched numerals'
300 / 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800. So the drawing's scale is confirmed by its own printed rule before any
drop was read from it. The widths written into this file are the **rule's exact values**, not the measured
ones; only the drops are measured.

The shipped `VCOG16-300BLK` file agrees exactly: its centre crosshair is also **16.6 MOA** wide (±8.3)
and **1.0 MOA** thick. Its separate 16.9 MOA figure is a different quantity — the spacing of that
reticle's horizontal ranging stadia, which this 5.56 variant does not carry at all.

## The frame

Framed to the ladder rather than to the glass: the etched **duplex posts** (inner ends near 46 MOA) and
the **ocular field circle** lie outside this frame and are not drawn, as does the heavy lower post about
90 MOA down.

## Wind holds

None — this reticle has no wind marks at all. Windage is held on the stadia and the crosshair.
