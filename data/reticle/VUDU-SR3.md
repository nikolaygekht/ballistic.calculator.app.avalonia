# VUDU-SR3 — EOTech Vudu SR-3, 5.56 (FFP, milliradian)

The 5.56 sibling of [SR-2](VUDU-SR2.md): the same speed ring and dot, a flatter three-rung ladder, and a ring
that is **open at the bottom** rather than closed. **First focal plane**, and its **load is printed on the
drawing**.

## The pattern

- **Speed ring** — **2.15 mrad outer diameter**, **0.3 mrad** thick, **open at the bottom** so the ladder is
  not obscured. Illuminated red.
- **Centre dot** — **0.44 mrad (1.5 MOA)**.
- **Reference ticks** on the horizontal at **4 mrad (13.75 MOA)** and **6 mrad (20.63 MOA)** each side,
  standing alone with **no connecting horizontal line**.
- **Three BDC bars**, **0.07 mrad (0.24 MOA)** thick, numbered 4, 5, 6, joined by a **vertical spine from the
  "4" bar to the "6" bar** only.

## The marks

| Below centre | Range | Numeral |
|---|---|---|
| **2.0 mrad** (6.88 MOA) | 400 yd | 4 |
| **3.0 mrad** (10.31 MOA) | 500 yd | 5 |
| **4.1 mrad** (14.09 MOA) | 600 yd | 6 |

All printed, in both units, with their yardages. Ranges are in **yards**.

Against SR-2 the ladder is shallower at every range — 2.0 mrad at 400 yd versus 2.5 — which is what a 75 gr
5.56 at 2900 ft/s does against a 175 gr .308 at 2550.

## The ring's opening — resolved

EOTech's horizontal **2 mrad (6.88 MOA)** callout **is** the width of the ring's opening: its extension lines
drop from the ring's two lower tips. Reproduced exactly — the opening measures **2.00 mrad across the outer
corners**, which puts the ring ends **21.6° below the horizontal** and makes the gap 136.9° of arc.

This was initially flagged as ambiguous, on the reasoning that a 2 mrad chord cannot fit across a 2.15 mrad
ring. It can: at the *outer* radius of 1.075 mrad the chord reaches 2 mrad just below the horizontal
diameter. The catalogue image settled it.

## One value not printed

**The BDC bars' width** — thickness is given (0.07 mrad), length is not. Drawn **1.0 mrad**.

Note the drawing is a **schematic, not to scale**: its ring is drawn far larger relative to the 4 and 6 mrad
tick spacing than the dimensions allow, so proportions in the picture cannot be measured. Only printed values
were used.
