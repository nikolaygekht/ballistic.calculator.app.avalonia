# M-16 Iron 3 Inch Eye Relief — M16 aperture and post

Not a scope. This is the **iron sight picture** of an M16 rear aperture with the front post inside it,
drawn as a reticle so it can be viewed and measured on the same footing as the optics here.

Field of view 350 × 350 MOA — two orders of magnitude wider than a scope reticle, because an aperture
at 3 in of eye relief subtends an enormous angle.

## The pattern

- A filled black field with a 172 MOA circular hole punched in it — the rear aperture, as the eye sees
  it at 3 in of eye relief.
- The **front post**: an 11 MOA wide rectangle rising 175 MOA from the centre.
- A dotted 60 MOA circle for reference.

The name records the eye relief the geometry was drawn for; the reticle's `name` attribute inside the
file says 2 in, which is a leftover — the file name is the one to trust.

## The marks

**None.** `<bdc/>` is empty. Iron sights compensate by moving the rear aperture, not by offering marks
to hold on, so there is nothing to label.
