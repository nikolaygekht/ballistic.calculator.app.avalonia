# HURON-BDC-HUNTER-6 — Trijicon Huron 1-6×24, BDC Hunter Holds

The **1-6×24** cut of Trijicon's hunting BDC (**HR624-C-2700008**). Geometrically it is the
[1-4×24 file](HURON-BDC-HUNTER-4.md) with the holds recomputed a hair shallower — see below.

**Second focal plane**, subtensions true at **6×** ("Measurements shown at highest magnification").

## The pattern

- **Fine cross** — **0.5 MOA** lines out to the post shoulders at **15 MOA**.
- **Heavy duplex posts** — **2.5 MOA** thick, left, right and bottom; the arm above centre stays fine.
- **Ticks** at **±5 and ±10 MOA**, 2.5 MOA tall.
- **Three hold bars** below the centre.

## Why this file exists at all

It is a fair question, because the difference from the 1-4×24 is **0.02 and 0.03 MOA** — under 0.4%, and
far below anything you could hold or measure. At 300 m, 0.03 MOA is about 2.6 mm.

It is kept as its own file because those are Trijicon's own **published** numbers for this part, not a
rounding I introduced, and because the file maps to a part number. But treat the two as the same reticle in
practice: if you own the 1-6×, loading the 1-4× file changes nothing you could see. This is a different
situation from the two 5.56 VCOG part numbers, where Trijicon published a **byte-identical** drawing for
both and only one file was made — here the sheets genuinely differ, if barely.
