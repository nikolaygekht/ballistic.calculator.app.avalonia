# H58 — H58-style grid, high-magnification view

A dense mrad grid in the style of the Horus H58. Field of view 21 × 21 mrad, zero 10.5 mrad from the
left edge and 8.5 mrad from the top — the extra room is below the crosshair, where the grid is.

The largest file here (~340 line elements), because the whole point of the pattern is a fine measurable
grid rather than a few marks.

## The pattern

- Fine horizontal line across the full 21 mrad; the vertical line runs **up only** from the centre —
  below the centre the spine is replaced by the tree.
- Hash marks every **0.5 mrad** on the horizontal, alternating tall (whole mrad, 0.15 mrad) and short
  (half mrad, 0.075 mrad), numbered every whole milliradian out to 6 on both sides.
- Below the centre, a **christmas tree** to −11 mrad: at every whole milliradian a horizontal bar
  carrying wind hashes every **0.2 mrad**, widening with depth — ±1.4 mrad at −1 and −2, ±1.6 at −3
  and −4, ±2.0 at −5 and −6, ±2.4 at −7 and −8, ±2.8 at −9 and −10, ±3.0 at −11. The half-milliradian
  rows in between carry a single short tick left of the spine.
- Numerals 1…10 down the tree, so a hold is read as a grid intersection rather than dialled.

## The marks

Geometric — the `<bdc>` anchors are on **every 2 mrad down to −10**:

| Position |
|---|
| −2, −4, −6, −8, −10 mrad |

Load-independent by design. The overlay labels each with the range your current trajectory crosses it,
which for a grid reticle is exactly how it is meant to be used: read the range off the solution, hold
at the grid line.
