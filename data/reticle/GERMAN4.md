# GERMAN4 — German #4

A classic European hunting picture. Field of view 60 × 60 MOA, zero in the centre.

## The pattern

- Bounding circle at 30 MOA radius.
- Fine cross across the full field.
- Heavy posts (4 MOA wide) on the left, right and **bottom** arms, from 15 MOA out to the field stop —
  the German #4 signature: three posts, open top, so nothing hides the target above the aiming point.
- A small dark-green centre dot, 0.3 MOA radius.

## The marks

**None.** `<bdc/>` is empty, and that is not a defect: a hunting reticle nominates no hold-over points.
The centre dot is the aiming point, the posts draw the eye to it, and there is nothing to label.

The *Far BDC* / *Near BDC* overlays simply draw nothing for this file. Use the Trajectory table for
holds.
