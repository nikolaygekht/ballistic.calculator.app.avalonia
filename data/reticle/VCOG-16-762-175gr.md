# VCOG-16-762-175gr — Trijicon VCOG 1-6×24, 7.62 segmented-circle BDC

The 7.62 member of the VCOG segmented-circle family (**VC16-C-1600004**, 7.62 / 175 gr): the same four
red arcs and wide red centre crosshair as the 5.56, with a **deeper eight-rung ladder out to 1000 m**.
**First focal plane**, framed at **6×** as Trijicon's sheet draws it.

## The pattern

- **Segmented circle** — four filled red arcs, **66.4 MOA between the outer corners**, with **17.8 MOA
  gaps** across both axes.
- **Centre crosshair** — red, **16.6 MOA wide** = **19 in at 100 m**, and the 100 m zero. Thickness
  **1.0 MOA**.
- **BDC stadia** — eight horizontal bars, each **19 in wide at its own range**, numerals in hundreds of
  metres.

Identical central geometry to the 5.56 file; only the ladder differs, which is as it should be — the
segmented circle is a fixed ranging feature and the ladder is the load-specific part.

Against the 5.56 ladder these are all deeper at the same range — 300 m at 5.65 MOA versus 4.38, 800 m at
33.47 versus 40.46. Note the crossover: the 7.62 starts steeper but overtakes, because the 175 gr match
bullet holds velocity far better past 500 m. That the two drawings differ in exactly this way is the best
evidence that Trijicon's per-part sheets are real ladders and not stock art — which is what makes the
identical pair of 5.56 sheets ([see that file](VCOG-16-556-55gr.md)) worth flagging.

## Which numbers came from where

Printed: the 16.6 MOA crosshair, 1.0 MOA thickness, 17.8 MOA gaps, 66.4 MOA outer span, and the three
notes defining the scheme (stadia in hundreds of metres, each 19 in wide at its range, crosshair 19 in at
100 m).

**No drop table is published**, so the stadia positions are measured off the sheet and then checked against
their own widths through the printed 19 in rule: measured widths of 5.51, 4.10, 3.23, 2.82 and 2.35 MOA give
301 / 405 / 514 / 588 / 705 m against etched 300 / 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 — the drawing's scale confirmed by
its own rule. The 800–1000 m bars are only 1.7–2.3 MOA wide, where one pixel is 0.07 MOA, so their widths
confirm range only to about ±30 m; their **positions** are what this file uses, and the widths written in are
the rule's exact values.

## The frame

Framed to the ladder: the etched duplex posts (inner ends near 46 MOA), the ocular field circle, and the
heavy lower post about 84 MOA down all lie outside this frame.
