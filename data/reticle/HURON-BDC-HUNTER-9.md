# HURON-BDC-HUNTER-9 — Trijicon Huron 3-9×40, BDC Hunter Holds

The **3-9×40** cut of Trijicon's hunting BDC (**HR940-C-2700006**) — the same pattern as the
[1-4×24](HURON-BDC-HUNTER-4.md) drawn with much finer strokes, as suits a higher-magnification scope.

**Second focal plane**, subtensions true at **9×** ("Measurements shown at highest magnification").

## The pattern

- **Fine cross** — **0.25 MOA** lines (half the 1-4×'s), out to the post shoulders at **15 MOA**.
- **Heavy duplex posts** — **1 MOA** thick (against 2.5 on the 1-4×), left, right and bottom; the arm above
  centre stays fine.
- **Ticks** at **±5 and ±10 MOA**, 1 MOA tall.
- **Three hold bars** below the centre.

The finer strokes are the whole point of this variant: at 9× a 2.5 MOA post would swallow the target. This
is the version to load if your scope is a mid-magnification hunting optic.

## The marks

| Drop below centre | Hold |
|---|---|
| 2.24 MOA | first |
| 5.00 MOA | second |
| 8.32 MOA | third |

Identical to the 1-4×24's holds. Trijicon states no ranges and no load — the application supplies both
from your own trajectory.

## Also the closest match for three more models

Trijicon's 2.5-10×40 (HR1040-C-2700002), 3-12×40 (HR1240-C-2700003) and 4-16×50 (HR1650-C-2700009) carry
the same holds with **0.2 MOA** fine lines rather than 0.25. That 0.05 MOA difference is not worth its own
file, so this one stands in for them. The family table is in the [1-4×24 file](HURON-BDC-HUNTER-4.md).

Note the 3-9×40 body also ships with a **German #4** (HR940-C-2700004) and a **Standard Duplex**
(HR940-C-2700005). Only `-2700006` is this reticle — the part number matters more than the scope here.

## Nothing was measured

Sheet stamped "**\*Reticles not drawn to scale**"; every dimension above is printed. The hold bars' width
(1.4 MOA here) is not printed and is drawn to match the sheet's proportions. The post taper where the heavy
sections meet the fine lines is not reproduced.
