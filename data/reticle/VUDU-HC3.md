# VUDU-HC3 — EOTech Vudu HC3 (SFP at 8×, MOA)

EOTech's quad-level BDC for 3-gun: a plain duplex with four holdover bars and nothing else. **Second focal
plane** — the subtensions are true at **8×** only, which is why the magnification is in the reticle name.

## The pattern

- **Centre dot** — **0.5 MOA**, illuminated red.
- **Horizontal** — a plain **0.25 MOA** line with **no graduation whatsoever**, closed by heavy posts. There
  are no horizontal subtensions on this reticle.
- **Upper vertical** — **0.5 MOA** thick.
- **Lower stem** — **0.25 MOA** thick, with a heavy post at the bottom.
- **Four holdover bars**, each **2 MOA** wide and **0.25 MOA** thick.

## The marks

| Below centre |
|---|
| **2 MOA** |
| **5 MOA** |
| **8.5 MOA** |
| **12.5 MOA** |

All four printed. They match EOTech's product copy exactly — "2-, 5-, 8.5- and 12.5-MOA subtensions on the
vertical axis at 8X" — which is what confirms the drawing is HC3 and not one of the HC1 variants whose tabs
sit beside it on the reticle index.

## The vertical stem changes thickness

**0.5 MOA above the centre, 0.25 MOA below.** That is what the drawing dimensions, and it is not a duplex
taper — it is a step at the centre. Unusual, so worth stating: if it looks wrong in the app, check this
before assuming a mistake elsewhere.

## Which numbers came from where

The **reticle-index drawing, supplied by Nikolay**. HC3 is the only one of the ten Vudu reticles with no
reticle manual: its scope manual (`Vudu_1-8x24SFP_RevB.pdf`, 24 pp) mentions "HC3" exactly once, in the turret
table, and contains no reticle figure. A dedicated HC3 manual was searched for across EOTech's own paths and
two dealer mirrors and does not appear to exist, though HC1 and HC2 manuals both do.

**Not published:** where the heavy posts begin. The drawing dimensions nothing horizontally except the line
thickness, so the frame here — graduation-free line to ±16 MOA, posts to ±20 — is our choice, the same
situation as the ACOG files.
